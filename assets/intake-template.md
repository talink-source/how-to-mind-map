# How-to mind map: intake

Fill this in once and attach it, along with any files listed in section 5, when you ask Claude to build your map. Short answers are fine. Skip anything you don't know; Claude will mark those spots as open questions in the map instead of interviewing you.

## 1. The outcome you own

Finish the sentence: **How to ___**

(For example: "How to drive growth for a company," "How to hire great engineers," "How to close enterprise deals.")

What does that outcome mean in practice? Name two or three concrete parts.

(For growth: get more customers, keep them longer, get them to spend more.)

## 2. Whose work this map describes

Pick one:

- [ ] My own business: work my company does for its own customers
- [ ] My clients: work I do to help other companies
- [ ] My team: work my team does inside a company

## 3. How you already break the work down

List the big moves you already use, in your own words. A framework, a set of phases, the sections of your usual deck or proposal. Two or three levels is plenty.

## 4. The calls you make on instinct

List decisions you make quickly that other people get wrong or take much longer on. One line each is enough. These become the deepest, most valuable parts of the map.

## 5. Material to attach

Attach whatever you have. Claude builds the map from your real methods first and fills gaps with general practice only where nothing of yours covers it.

- Frameworks, playbooks or SOPs (standard operating procedures)
- Decks, talks or workshop materials
- Proposals, audits, reports or other past deliverables (remove anything confidential)
- Templates, checklists, scripts or email sequences you reuse
- Posts or articles you've written
- Notes or transcripts from calls where you explained how you work

## 6. Your words

Terms you always use, and terms you never want to see.

## 7. Leave out

Anything confidential, any client or company names that shouldn't appear, and any topics outside the scope of this map.

## 8. Who will use the map

- [ ] Just me, to see my own expertise and decide what to hand to AI first
- [ ] My team, to learn how the work is done
- [ ] Clients or an audience, as a teaching asset

## 9. Anything else

Practitioners whose thinking you want reflected (they won't be named in the map), a deadline, or a branch you most want to go deep on.
